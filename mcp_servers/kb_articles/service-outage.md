# Checking Service Status During an Outage

If the app or website seems down, check the current service status first before assuming it's a problem specific to your account.

During an active outage, most features may be slow or unavailable, but your data is never lost — outages affect availability, not stored data. Updates are typically posted every 15-30 minutes until resolved.

If service status shows "operational" but you're still having trouble, the issue is more likely specific to your device or account, not a wider outage.
